My website iluxuryegypt.com has a major image display problem on mobile devices.

THE PROBLEM:
Background/hero images and section images are being cropped randomly and show only a small portion of the image instead of the full image scaled to fit the screen. On mobile, users see a zoomed-in, partial view of images instead of the complete image like they would see on a desktop/laptop screen.

WHAT I WANT:
Every image and background image on the site should display its FULL content, scaled and centered to fit the container, similar to how a desktop browser shows it — just smaller. The user should see the complete image (not a cropped fragment), regardless of screen size.

REQUIRED FIXES:
1. Go through every hero section, background image, and <img> tag on the site (homepage and all pages).

2. For background images (CSS background-image), make sure they use:
   background-size: contain or background-size: cover combined with background-position: center center, and test both to choose whichever shows the full meaningful content of the image without cropping out important parts (like the boat, the pyramid, the text overlay area).

3. For <img> tags, use:
   width: 100%;
   height: auto;
   object-fit: contain; (or "cover" only if the image still shows its full subject after cropping)

4. Make sure hero sections have a min-height that adapts to mobile screens (use vh units or aspect-ratio) so images aren't forced into oddly cropped fixed-height containers on small screens.

5. Check the slider/carousel components specifically (the hero image sliders on the homepage) — these are showing cropped, off-center images on mobile. Fix their container sizing and image positioning so the full image is visible and centered.

6. Test on a mobile viewport (375px width) and confirm: every image shows its complete content (not cut off at edges), exactly like it would appear on a desktop browser, just resized proportionally.

Go through each page (Home, About, Stay, Testimonials, Travel Guide, FAQs, etc.) and fix this systematically — don't just fix the homepage.
